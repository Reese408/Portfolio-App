## Why I Love Docker

Docker has transformed how I build and deploy applications. The ability to containerize everything and move it seamlessly between development, testing, and production environments eliminates the "it works on my machine" problem entirely. For cybersecurity classes, Docker's isolation capabilities are perfect for testing tools and scenarios without risk to my host system. In production, knowing that my containerized application will run identically anywhere gives me confidence in deployments. Docker has become an indispensable tool I use every single day.
