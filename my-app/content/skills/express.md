## Why I Love Express

Express is lean, fast, and gets out of your way. Its middleware-based architecture is elegant - I can compose functionality exactly how I need it without framework bloat. Building APIs with Express feels natural and intuitive. The huge ecosystem of middleware means I can add features like authentication, logging, or CORS with just a few lines of code. In the GamingHub project, Express handled API requests seamlessly while Socket.io managed real-time connections - they work perfectly together. Express is my go-to because it's simple but powerful enough for any backend need.
