## Why I Love Next.js

Next.js provides an incredible developer experience with sensible defaults and powerful features out of the box. The App Router's flexibility to mix server and client components makes building performant applications intuitive. The framework handles routing, optimization, and deployment seamlessly, letting me focus on building features rather than configuration. Combined with TypeScript, it's my ideal stack for modern web development.
